About Bun Blocks

Bun Blocks is a free puzzle game made by an independent developer. It is an original game: easy to learn, quick to play and hard to master. You can play it in any modern browser on a phone, tablet or computer, and you can add it to your home screen to play like an app, even without a connection.

What makes it different

  • Fair for everyone. The pieces you receive never depend on how well you are doing. There is no secret difficulty that changes to help or hurt you, and the same pieces are given to every player in the daily and the weekly cup.
  • Scores you can trust. Scores posted to the leaderboard are checked by the server, which replays every move. A score that did not come from a real, legal game is rejected.
  • No pressure. There are no timers and no lives. Take as long as you want over a move.
  • Light on data. You can play without an account. Signing in is optional, and anonymous usage statistics are off unless you say yes.

Who makes it

Bun Blocks is designed and built by one person, and the rabbit on the map is its mascot. New modes, levels and themes are added regularly, based on what players ask for. If you have an idea or found a problem, please get in touch from the contact page.

Paying for the game

The game is free to play. To keep it running, there may be optional extras, such as colour themes and coin packs, and there may be ads. Extras are cosmetic or save you time; they never change the rules, the pieces or the leaderboard. You can read exactly what is stored and why on the privacy page.
